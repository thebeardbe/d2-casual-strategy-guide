## Map data and wording

The area layout rules, map-type classification, per-map wording and the example
screenshots come from:

> **The Code to Navigate Every Diablo 2 Map** — WithNoFiller
> <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J1EFUYGk4CA> (2022)

The area level figures come from PureDiablo's area level table
(<https://www.purediablo.com/diablo-2/diablo-2-area-levels>), which also records
where Diablo II: Resurrected differs from Lord of Destruction (for example
Underground Passage Level 2 at area level 85 instead of 83).

The turn rules, the waypoint observations and the caveats are paraphrased from
that video. Every area card links to the timestamp it came from, so a reader can
check any claim against the source.

## Builds (planned)

Build pages are planned to be sourced from MrLlamaSC's leveling guides and guided
playthroughs, with a timestamp for every claim. Nothing from those videos is
reproduced verbatim; the pages are summaries with links back.

## Corrections

If anything here is wrong, or a source is miscredited, open an issue. Corrections
are preferred over quiet edits, and corrections to the source video's own mistakes
should say so on the page.
